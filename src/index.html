  1. Use desktop Chrome or Edge and click Choose flight folder.

About XC distances

The highest estimated Swiss CCC geometry score selects the route and its distance: open ×1.0, straight ×1.2, flat triangle ×1.2, FAI triangle ×1.4. ≈ means the search remains time-limited. Calculate missing scores explicitly; results are saved in flightbook-xc.json. Standard searches allow about 1 second per category, refinement 5 seconds per category, plus preparation. Manual distances remain manually entered open routes without inferred points. No takeoff trimming or official contest validation.
