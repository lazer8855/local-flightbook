Connect your files

  1. Use desktop Chrome or Edge and click Choose flight folder.
  2. Select the main folder containing your flights and approve read access. Every subfolder is scanned, whatever its name.
  3. Click Choose Europe map and select europe.pmtiles.
  4. For a manual flight, choose its save location and approve write access if asked.

Repeat these selections after reopening. Your IGC files are never changed.

How XC distances are calculated

Open XC optimizes start, up to three turnpoints and finish. Free triangles require a closing gap within 20% of the perimeter; FAI triangles also require each leg to be at least 28% of the perimeter. Triangle distances subtract the closing gap. Calculations use all valid fixes with WGS84 distances and no automatic launch/landing trimming. ≈ marks a time-limited estimate; Refine XC distances searches longer. Not found yet is not proof that no triangle exists. Manual flights have no XC distance. These are local distance estimates, not official contest scores.
